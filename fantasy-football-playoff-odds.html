What you get

  • Projected playoff bracketCurrent and projected seeds in a bracket that follows your league's playoff settings, with the third-place game shown separately.
  • Consolation bracketIf your league runs one, see who's headed to the consolation bracket too.
  • Simulated oddsEach team's playoff chances, simulated from the remaining schedule and projections.
  • Playoff pushStandings-based bubble signals across your leagues, playoff-week matchups and potential RB handcuffs. Signals are not clinch or elimination calculations.

How it works

  1. Connect your league and open Playoff Scenarios.
  2. Check the bracket, then switch to scenarios or playoff push from the view menu.
  3. Share a playoff card with your league chat.

Questions

How are odds calculated?

The remaining schedule is simulated many times using weekly projections. It's an estimate; actual results will vary.

Does it follow my league's playoff format?

It uses your league's playoff team count and start week when your provider exposes them.
