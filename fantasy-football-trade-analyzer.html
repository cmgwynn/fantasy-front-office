What you get

  • Fairness checkCompare the value going each way, with a clear verdict instead of a raw number.
  • Lineup impactSee how your starting lineup changes after the deal, this week and for the rest of the season.
  • Works around kickoff locksIf a player in the deal has already kicked off, you still get the fairness and rest-of-season view.
  • Uses your league's rostersPick players straight from any team in your league.

How it works

  1. Connect your league and open the Trade Analyzer.
  2. Choose the team you're trading with and add players to each side.
  3. Review the verdict and lineup impact before you accept or counter.

Questions

Is the verdict a guarantee?

No. It's an estimate based on projections and roster fit. Your league's context still matters.

Can I analyze trades in ESPN leagues?

Yes, as long as the league is viewable to the public so its rosters can be loaded.
