Fantasy FO, on your home screen

Open your fantasy tools in their own app window. No app store download or separate account required.

Install app

If your browser doesn't offer installation, use the steps below.

iPhone or iPad

Open fantasyfo.com in Safari. Tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App enabled if offered, then tap Add. Look under More or Edit Actions if the option is hidden.

Android

Open fantasyfo.com in Chrome. Tap Install app above when available, or open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Confirm the installation.

Desktop

In Chrome or Edge, use the install icon in the address bar or the browser's app-install menu. On supported versions of Safari for Mac, choose File then Add to Dock. Installation options depend on your browser and operating system.

Online data, local plans

Scores, projections, and league connections need the internet. The offline screen will not show old recommendations as live. Updates arrive through the same website; reopen the app online to load the latest release.

Local saves may be separate between the browser and installed app, especially on Apple devices. You may need to reconnect your league. Plans do not automatically sync across devices and can be lost when app or site data is cleared. Uninstalling the icon may not clear browser data; see Privacy for removal instructions.

This is an installable web app, not an Apple App Store or Google Play listing. For help, support@fantasyfo.com.
