What you get

  • Connect with a league IDPaste the number after leagueId= in your ESPN league's address bar. Add several, separated by commas.
  • Uses ESPN rosters and custom scoringRosters, matchups and standings come from ESPN. Supported scoring rules and position overrides are applied to available projected stats. The app identifies unsupported rules and missing inputs instead of labeling incomplete estimates verified.
  • Mix with SleeperSee your ESPN matchups next to your Sleeper leagues on Gameday.
  • No password neededFantasy Front Office never asks for your ESPN login.

How it works

  1. Open your league on espn.com/fantasy and copy the leagueId from the address bar.
  2. Choose ESPN in Fantasy Front Office and paste the ID.
  3. If the league is private, ask the league manager whether making it publicly viewable is appropriate. This exposes read-only league information to others; do not change that privacy setting without their agreement.

Questions

Why won't my ESPN league load?

Private ESPN leagues can't be read without a login. Ask your league manager to make the league viewable to the public, then try again.

Is this affiliated with ESPN?

No. Fantasy Front Office is an independent tool and isn't affiliated with ESPN, Sleeper or Yahoo.
