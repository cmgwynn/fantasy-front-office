Reporting a problem

Include the tool name, fantasy provider, selected week, device/browser, and what you expected versus what happened. A redacted screenshot helps. Only include a league ID if you are comfortable sharing it privately with support.

Do not send passwords, session cookies, payment information, or unredacted private league exports. Hide personal details and other managers' information from screenshots.

Privacy and rights concerns

Use the same email for privacy questions or concerns about photos, logos, or other materials. Include the relevant page or resource and enough detail to investigate. See our Privacy notice and Terms of use.

About the site

Fantasy Front Office is an independent fantasy football decision-support site operated by Chavous Gwynn for United States adults aged 18 and older. Support is handled by the operator; immediate responses are not guaranteed. Submit time-sensitive roster moves through your fantasy provider, not by email.
