Fantasy FO beside your league

The side panel keeps Fantasy FO docked in the same browser window as Sleeper, ESPN, or CBS. Set your lineup or place claims on the provider page while your Inbox, Lineup, and Waivers tools stay open next to it.

Sleeper, ESPN, and CBS block other sites from embedding their pages, so a browser side panel is the only way to show both in one window.

Install (Chrome or Edge on a computer)

  1. Download the side panel (.zip) and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped fantasy-fo-side-panel folder.
  5. Pin the Fantasy FO icon from the puzzle-piece menu.
  6. Open your league, then click the Fantasy FO icon. Use Open league in the panel to switch your current tab to your league or draft.

A Chrome Web Store version is coming, which will install with one click.

What it can access

The extension only shows fantasyfo.com in the side panel and, when you click Open league, changes the address of your current tab. It cannot read provider pages, cookies, or passwords. See the privacy policy.

Phones and tablets don't support browser side panels. Use split screen there, or install the app.
