Player Analytics

Compare stats and model projections for your scoring. Not expert-consensus rankings.

HOW PLAYER ANALYTICS & TRADE VALUE ARE CALCULATED
• TPRR % (Targets per Route Run): (Targets/Gm ÷ Estimated Routes Run/Gm) × 100 derived from actual offensive snaps (off_snp).
• YPRR (Yards per Route Run): Receiving Yards/Gm (rec_yd) ÷ Estimated Routes Run/Gm.
• YPTOE (Yards per Target Over Expected): (Rec Yards ÷ Targets) - Positional NFL Baseline (8.3 WR / 7.4 TE).
• RB Gold Touches / Gm: Red Zone Carries/Gm (rush_rz_att) + Red Zone Targets/Gm (rec_rz_tgt).
• RYOE / Gm (Rush Yards Over Expected): (Player YPC - 4.15 NFL Baseline YPC) × Rush Attempts/Gm.
• QB CPOE & EPA/Play: Completion % (pass_cmp ÷ pass_att) - 64.2% Expected and efficiency per dropback from yards, TDs, and INTs.
• Trade Value (0–9999): Exponential player-rank curve (7800 × e^(-0.0105×Rank)) blended with PPG production ratio (PPG ÷ Baseline × 3400), Superflex/TEP multipliers, and Dynasty Age Curve.

How rankings & ratings work

Startable minimums: QB 14, RB/WR 8, TE 6 projected points, inclusive. Show all keeps lower projections below those minimums and labels them low output. The top 12 are ranked by projection with 25% of positional defensive allowance deviation, capped at ±10%; this is illustrative, not a new projection or calibrated probability. Projections may already include matchup. Higher matchup ratings are easier: 0–100 percentile of fantasy points allowed to the entire position, using up to six prior completed NFL weeks. At least three games per defense and 24 qualifying defenses are required. Standard/half/PPR scoring only; custom bonuses are not included. Descriptive, not opponent-strength adjusted; not a receiver-versus-cornerback grade. Injuries, role changes and weather can outweigh the matchup. No seeded schedule grades are used here.
