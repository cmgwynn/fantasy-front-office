What is and isn't included?

One active league snapshot, its completed-result cache, loaded player data, selected roster/week/view, and all recognized local app saves are included. Other leagues' live rosters, provider sign-ins, cookies, notification permissions, live score refresh, external schedule caches, and unsaved trade/bracket scenarios are not. Multiweek scenarios are included; future scores and bye snapshots retain their original uncertainty. Backups preserve the model inputs/results as saved, not verified historical outcomes. Snapshots are manual, not continuous autosave. Browser clearing or storage eviction can remove the device copy; keep a downloaded file too.

HOW PLAYER ANALYTICS & TRADE VALUE ARE CALCULATED
• TPRR % (Targets per Route Run): (Targets/Gm ÷ Estimated Routes Run/Gm) × 100 derived from actual offensive snaps (off_snp).
• YPRR (Yards per Route Run): Receiving Yards/Gm (rec_yd) ÷ Estimated Routes Run/Gm.
• YPTOE (Yards per Target Over Expected): (Rec Yards ÷ Targets) - Positional NFL Baseline (8.3 WR / 7.4 TE).
• RB Gold Touches / Gm: Red Zone Carries/Gm (rush_rz_att) + Red Zone Targets/Gm (rec_rz_tgt).
• RYOE / Gm (Rush Yards Over Expected): (Player YPC - 4.15 NFL Baseline YPC) × Rush Attempts/Gm.
• QB CPOE & EPA/Play: Completion % (pass_cmp ÷ pass_att) - 64.2% Expected and efficiency per dropback from yards, TDs, and INTs.
• Trade Value (0–9999): Exponential player-rank curve (7800 × e^(-0.0105×Rank)) blended with PPG production ratio (PPG ÷ Baseline × 3400), Superflex/TEP multipliers, and Dynasty Age Curve.
