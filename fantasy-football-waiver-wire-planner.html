What you get

  • Roster needs firstEach position gets a priority from your starters, depth, injuries and byes, so the top suggestions fill real gaps.
  • FAAB bid suggestionsQueue claims with a suggested bid and drop candidate, and keep track of how much budget you've committed.
  • Waiver day across leaguesSee potential same-position upgrades across leagues using the active league's scoring. Review each league's lineup and roster rules before claiming.
  • Your plan stays savedQueued claims are saved in your browser, one list per league, until you submit them in your fantasy app.

How it works

  1. Connect your league and open the Waiver Planner.
  2. Filter by position and review the roster-need suggestions.
  3. Queue claims with bids, then submit them on Sleeper or ESPN before waivers run.

Questions

Does it submit claims for me?

No. It helps you plan; claims are submitted in your fantasy app.

Does it know who is available in my league?

Yes, availability comes from your league's rosters as of the last refresh. Always confirm in your app before claiming.
