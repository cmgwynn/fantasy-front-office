Privacy notice

Effective November 2, 2026

Fantasy Front Office at fantasyfo.com is operated by Chavous Gwynn for United States adults aged 18 and older. Contact support@fantasyfo.com with privacy questions.

League information and browser storage

The tools use the account username or league IDs you enter to request information from fantasy providers. Depending on the provider and tool, this includes manager names, rosters, league settings, matchups, scores, transactions, and draft or league history. Yahoo roster files and other supported imports are read in your browser; the app does not upload those files to a Fantasy Front Office server.

The app uses localStorage and IndexedDB on your device to remember connection fields, league snapshots, player-data caches, selected views, draft and waiver plans, watchlists, and recommendation feedback. This is local browser storage, not a Fantasy Front Office cloud account. Data may remain until you clear it or your browser removes it. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to access it.

To remove local saves, close the site and use your browser's site settings to clear site data for fantasyfo.com. This removes saved plans and connection information. If you used the old fantasy-front-office.pages.dev address, clear that site's data separately. Clearing browser data does not delete information held by fantasy providers or previously sent support emails.

Third-party requests and hosting

The installable app uses the same website and data sources. Its service worker saves only a generic offline page in Cache Storage; it does not cache league responses, scores, or recommendations. Browser caching and the local saves described above are separate. Installed apps may have their own storage, particularly on Apple devices. Clear their app/site data separately when removing saves.

Your browser requests football and league data directly from Sleeper and ESPN, and loads player photos and team logos from their content services. Those services receive your IP address and the requested resource; requests for account or league information include the relevant username or identifier. Their own privacy policies apply: Sleeper and Disney/ESPN.

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this site and handles support-email forwarding. Hosting and network services may process IP addresses, request URLs, timestamps, browser information, and operational or security logs. A URL can include a username or league ID. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Local storage does not mean your use of the site is anonymous.

Shared links

Connection links, including those created by Copy share link, may contain your account username or league ID and selected week. Anyone receiving a link can see those values and may be able to load publicly available league information. Remove the connection parameters or share only https://fantasyfo.com/ if you do not want to share those identifiers.

Side panel extension

The optional Fantasy Front Office Side Panel extension shows this site in your browser's side panel. It does not read provider pages, cookies, or passwords. When you choose to open your league, it changes the address of your current tab to that Sleeper, ESPN, or CBS page. It sends no data to the operator beyond normal requests to this site.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address, message, and any attachments are forwarded through Cloudflare to the operator's inbox and processed by the inbox provider. They are used to respond, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests, and retained as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Do not send passwords, session cookies, payment details, or sensitive personal information. Redact other managers' information from screenshots where possible.

Advertising and measurement

Cloudflare automatically adds a Web Analytics beacon to the hosted site to measure page visits and performance. According to Cloudflare's documentation, Web Analytics does not log URL query strings. This is separate from the hosting and security processing described above, which can involve request URLs. Browser privacy tools may block the beacon.

Connected tools are currently a US-only beta for adults 18 and older. Participation requires explicit agreement to limited usage measurement before connecting or importing a real league, reopening a saved real-league workspace, or using connected tools. This is a condition of beta participation, not optional analytics or a claim that measurement is technically necessary to sync a league. The agreement checkbox starts unchecked. Previous optional sharing choices alone do not enroll you in this beta. You can decline and use the fictional demo or public information pages instead. See the beta terms.

Cloudflare's request-country information determines US eligibility but is not written to our usage dataset. Non-US or unknown locations, Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals, and unavailable eligibility checks pause connected access; we do not override those signals. Checking service availability makes a request even without agreement, but does not write an event. No usage events are sent before agreement and confirmed eligibility. Once agreed, events may include unsuccessful connection attempts.

Usage events send only a fixed event name (such as connecting, a connection failure, opening a tool or reviewing a recommendation), provider type and tool name to our first-party Cloudflare endpoint. They do not include usernames, league IDs, roster contents, full URLs, error messages or visitor identifiers. Cloudflare processes network metadata to deliver requests. Events are stored in Cloudflare Analytics Engine under its retention policy; aggregate counts help evaluate onboarding and reliability, not individual activity. Counts are approximate and reflect participating browsers with successfully delivered events, not every user.

Your agreement, measurement choice and the last visit week with sharing enabled are stored locally. The week is used to report a return in a later seven-day reporting period without sending the week or a visitor ID. You may withdraw through Usage privacy in the footer. Withdrawal stops future events, removes the local visit week, and pauses syncing and connected tools without deleting saved plans. Already received events cannot be attributed to you for individual deletion. Browser privacy settings, blockers and delivery failures can prevent events from reaching the dataset.

The current site does not load advertising scripts. The Player Analytics tool analyzes football statistics, not visitor behavior. Browser saves are used for app functionality, not advertising profiles. We do not sell your league information or support messages. Before adding advertising or additional tracking, we will update this notice and implement any required consent controls.

Your choices and requests

You can use the site without connecting a league, avoid sharing connection links, and clear local site data. For questions or requests about information sent to support, email support@fantasyfo.com. We may need to verify a request using the sending email address. We cannot delete provider records or remotely clear your device's browser storage; contact the relevant provider for its records.

This site is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support, contact us. Updates to this notice will appear here with a revised effective date.
