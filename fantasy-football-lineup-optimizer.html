What you get

  • Built on your league's scoringProjections are scored with your league's settings, so PPR, half-PPR and custom bonuses count.
  • Injuries, byes and locksPlayers who are out, on bye or already locked by kickoff are handled automatically, so suggestions stay legal.
  • Clear moves, not a wall of numbersSee exactly which players to swap in and out, with projected points gained, and your unchanged starters grouped by position.
  • Every league in one placeSwitch between all of your connected leagues without reconnecting.

How it works

  1. Connect your Sleeper account or ESPN league.
  2. Open the Lineup Optimizer and pick the week.
  3. Review the suggested moves, then set your lineup on Sleeper or ESPN.

Questions

Will it change my lineup automatically?

No. Fantasy Front Office only reads league data. You make the moves in your fantasy app.

Where do projections come from?

Weekly player projections are pulled from public fantasy data and scored with your league's rules. Treat them as one input, not a guarantee.
